By using our website or submitting your information to us, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and the practices described below, subject to applicable law.

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with Celebso Startup School.

Personal Information

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile/phone number
  • City, state and country
  • Age or date of birth, where reasonably required
  • Educational qualifications
  • Professional information
  • Work experience
  • Resume/CV
  • LinkedIn or other professional profile
  • Portfolio or personal website
  • Profile photograph, where voluntarily provided

Application Information

If you apply for Celebso Startup School, we may collect:

  • Startup or business ideas
  • Entrepreneurial interests
  • Application responses
  • Essays or written answers
  • Career goals
  • Previous startup or professional experience
  • Projects and achievements
  • Portfolio information
  • Other information voluntarily submitted during the application process

Program Information

If you are selected or enrolled, we may collect:

  • Attendance records

  • Feedback
  • Participation records
  • Certificate/completion information
  • Program-related communications

  • Date and time of access

  • Website interaction information

This information may be used for website security, analytics, performance monitoring, and improvement of our Services.

We may use personal information for purposes including:

  • Processing applications;
  • Reviewing and evaluating applicants;
  • Selecting participants;
  • Communicating with applicants;
  • Providing the 45-Day Startup & Entrepreneurship Program;
  • Managing participant records, attendance, and participation;
  • Providing educational services;
  • Issuing certificates;
  • Responding to questions and support requests;
  • Organizing workshops, events, and community activities;
  • Improving our programs and website;
  • Conducting analytics and research;
  • Preventing fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity;
  • Maintaining security and complying with applicable laws; and
  • Sending relevant program, community, event, or promotional communications where legally permitted.

5. Startup Ideas and Business Information

Applicants and participants may voluntarily share startup ideas, business models, product concepts, project information, strategies, or other business-related information during the application or program.

Submitting such information to Celebso Startup School does not automatically transfer ownership of the participant's startup idea, business, product, invention, code, trademark, design, or other intellectual property to Celebso Technologies or Celebso Group.

Participants should avoid submitting highly confidential trade secrets or information that they are not authorized to disclose. If a separate written confidentiality or intellectual-property agreement applies, that agreement will govern the relevant information.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

We may process personal information on the basis of your consent, providing services requested by you, legitimate and lawful business purposes, compliance with applicable legal obligations, or other lawful grounds available under applicable law.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Celebso Startup School. These service providers may support: website hosting, cloud infrastructure, email and communication, application management, analytics, payment processing, event management, customer support, technology infrastructure, and security services.

8. Disclosure Required by Law

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, comply with lawful government or regulatory requests, protect rights, property, or safety of Celebso Technologies, Celebso Group, participants, or others, detect/prevent fraud, or enforce applicable Terms and agreements.
